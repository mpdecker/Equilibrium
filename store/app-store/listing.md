## Promotional text

Type how you feel — Equilibrium shapes a living ambient soundscape in real time.

## Description

Equilibrium generates evolving ambient music from your mood. Enter a short check-in and the app maps it into synthesis parameters — harmony, texture, motion, and color — then keeps the soundscape drifting gently over time.

Practice with quick mood prompts, run a timed Session with a soft sonic arc, and Reflect with journaling. Playback works offline from your last saved soundscape; journals and feedback sync when you are back online.

Built for mindfulness and emotional regulation support — not a medical device and not a substitute for professional care.
