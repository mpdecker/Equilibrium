## Full description

Equilibrium turns a short mood note into a living ambient soundscape. Generative audio (Tone.js / optional WASM worklet) evolves while you practice, run a timed session, or journal.

Features:
• Practice — quick mood check-ins that reshape the sound
• Session — timed listening with a gentle sonic arc
• Reflect — journaling with offline queue and sync
• Offline playback from your last saved soundscape

Not a medical device. For mindfulness and emotional regulation support only.
